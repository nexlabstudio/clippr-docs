# Billing

Manage your subscription, view usage, and update payment methods.

## Upgrade Your Plan

1. Go to **Organization Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **Upgrade**
3. Select your new plan
4. Choose monthly or yearly billing
5. Complete payment

Yearly billing saves 20% compared to monthly.

## View Usage

The billing page shows your current usage:

- Apps created vs. limit
- Links created vs. limit
- Team members vs. limit

## Payment Methods

### Add Payment Method

1. Go to **Billing** → **Payment Methods**
2. Click **Add Card**
3. Enter card details
4. Click **Save**

### Update Payment Method

1. Click on the existing payment method
2. Click **Update**
3. Enter new details

### Remove Payment Method

You can remove a payment method if you have another one on file.

## Invoices

View and download past invoices:

1. Go to **Billing** → **Invoices**
2. Click on an invoice to view
3. Click **Download PDF** if needed

## Cancel Subscription

1. Go to **Billing**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Confirm cancellation

After cancellation:
- Access continues until the end of the billing period
- Data is retained for 30 days
- You can reactivate anytime

## Reactivate Subscription

If you've cancelled:

1. Go to **Billing**
2. Click **Reactivate**
3. Select your plan
4. Confirm payment

## FAQ

### When am I charged?

You're charged at the start of each billing period (monthly or yearly).

### Can I change plans mid-cycle?

Yes. Upgrades are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

### What happens if I exceed my limits?

You'll receive a warning when approaching limits. At the limit, you won't be able to create new resources until you upgrade or the next billing period.

### Do you offer refunds?

Contact support within 14 days for refund requests.
