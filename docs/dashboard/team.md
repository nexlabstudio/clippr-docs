# Team Management

Collaborate with your team by inviting members to your organization.

## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full access, billing, delete org |
| **Admin** | Manage apps, links, members |
| **Member** | Create and manage links |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access |

## Invite a Member

1. Go to **Organization Settings** → **Members**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter their email address
4. Select their role
5. Click **Send Invite**

The invitee will receive an email with a link to join.

## Pending Invites

View and manage pending invitations:

- **Resend** - Send the invite email again
- **Cancel** - Revoke the invitation

## Manage Members

### Change Role

1. Find the member in the list
2. Click the role dropdown
3. Select the new role

### Remove Member

1. Click the menu (⋮) next to the member
2. Click **Remove**
3. Confirm removal

<Warning>
Removed members immediately lose access. Any links they created remain in the organization.
</Warning>

## Transfer Ownership

To transfer organization ownership:

1. Go to **Organization Settings**
2. Click **Transfer Ownership**
3. Select the new owner (must be an existing admin)
4. Confirm the transfer

## Best Practices

### Principle of Least Privilege

- Give members only the access they need
- Use Viewer role for stakeholders who only need reports
- Reserve Admin for team leads

### Offboarding

- Remove members promptly when they leave
- Rotate API keys if a member with API access leaves
- Review and update permissions periodically
